# Tacticular — Design Document v1.0

## 1. Goal

A chess-like, turn-based tactics game. It is as simple as XCOM and set in an original D&D-style
fantasy world. Tactics win and brute force loses: the player wins with information, position and timing.

| Pillar | How the rules give it |
|---|---|
| Chess-like | Visible numbers before every action, telegraphed enemy attacks, zones of control, no hidden modifiers |
| XCOM-simple | 2 AP, one rule for what ends a turn, a few statuses |
| D&D | d20 with advantage and disadvantage, saving throws, cooldown magic, fantasy setting |
| Tactics over force | Enemies outnumber you 3:1, fog, horns that call help, an AI that flanks, priority targets |

## 2. Mission

- Goal: kill all enemies. Loss: all soldiers die. Death is instant at 0 HP and lasts for the mission.
- Map: 24×16 to 32×24 tiles, square grid, random from a seed. There are at least 2 routes between any two regions.
- Forces: 4 soldiers against 3–4 groups of 2–4 enemies each.
- Start: the squad starts concealed at one edge. All enemies start Unaware and patrol or guard.

## 3. Round

```
Player phase → Enemy phase (groups act, closest to the squad first) → Round end
Round end: horns sound, statuses tick, cooldowns −1
```

## 4. Unit control

- 2 AP per unit. You can switch between units at any time in your phase.
- Rule: an action that targets an enemy ends the turn. Utility actions cost 1 AP. Stances end the turn.
- Exception: the Ranger's longbow needs 2 AP.

| 1 AP | Ends turn |
|---|---|
| Move (1 AP = blue range, 2 AP = yellow dash range) | Attack (shoot, melee, throw, attack spell) |
| Reload, open door (silent) | Fire flask |
| Distract (stone, noise 5, up to 6 tiles away) | Overwatch: first enemy that moves in sight, with disadvantage. A melee unit reacts only to adjacent tiles. |
| Heal, Ward, Bulwark, smoke bomb, Blink, Vanish, Rouse | Hunker: needs cover. Attacks against the unit have disadvantage, and it has advantage on Will saves. No reactions. |

- Undo: a move can be undone if it revealed no new tiles and caused no roll.
- Movement: diagonal steps cost 1, then 2, then 1, and so on.

## 5. Combat

- Attack: d20 + Aim ≥ Defense, where Defense = 10 + Dodge + cover (low +2, high +5).
- Advantage / disadvantage: roll 2d20 and keep the high or low one. Any advantage cancels any disadvantage.

| Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---|---|
| Target flanked | Overwatch or reaction shot |
| Target Unaware or surprised | Target in smoke or hunkered |
| | Attacker Suppressed, Frightened or Shaken |
| | Outside best range (also −1 Aim per extra tile) |

- Natural 20: crit, roll the damage dice twice. Natural 1: miss.
- Armor: reduces each hit by its value, minimum 1 damage.
- Cover: counts only if adjacent to the target, facing the attacker (within 90°). Otherwise the target is flanked.
- Peek: cover next to the shooter never blocks its own shots.
- Saves (mind magic): d20 + Will ≥ 10 + caster's Will. Mind magic ignores cover.
- Sure effects: heals, wards, Bulwark and fire flasks never roll.
- Fire flask: hits everyone in a 3×3 area, allies too. Low cover becomes open ground. High cover becomes low cover.
- Zone of control: a unit with a melee main weapon threatens adjacent tiles. A unit that leaves such a tile takes a free melee attack.
- Reactions resolve in this order: overwatch, then zone of control, then the move continues.

## 6. Information

- Vision: 8 tiles (Ranger 9, Rogue 10). Walls, high cover and closed doors block sight. Low cover and water do not.
- Fog: black (unexplored), grey (explored, with ghost markers of last known enemy positions and the round), color (visible).
- Intent: a seen Unaware enemy shows a patrol arrow. The Ogre and the Bomber mark their target one turn early. A seen enemy on overwatch shows its zone.
- Noise (radius from the source): stone 5, melee 4, bow / crossbow / Arcane Bolt 8, fire flask 16. Knives and daggers 0. Noise out of sight shows a "?" marker.
- Move preview icons: your cover, enemies you flank (green), enemies that flank you (red), detection and overwatch zones, zones of control.

## 7. Stealth and enemy states

```
Unaware ──(detects soldier)───────────────────────► Engaged
   │                                                  ▲
   └─(hears noise / sees ally die)─► Investigating ───┘
```

- Concealment belongs to each soldier. A concealed soldier is detected inside an enemy's line of sight and detection radius: 4 tiles when the enemy is Unaware, 6 when it is Investigating. Noise or an attack also breaks concealment. Only the Rogue's Vanish gives it back.
- Silent kill: if the target dies and no other enemy sees the attack, nobody is alerted.
- Reveal reaction: when the player reveals a group in the player phase, each enemy in it that is still alive gets 1 action, picked by the AI. Attacks in this action have disadvantage (surprise). This reaction triggers overwatch with a normal roll, because surprise cancels the overwatch disadvantage. It happens once per group.
- Horn: at round end, each group with an Engaged enemy alive alerts all groups within 15 tiles (Warchief: 30). Those groups become Investigating toward the caller's target.
- An enemy that sees an ally die but cannot see the attacker becomes Investigating toward the attacker.
- Combat AI: utility scoring of cover, flanks, hit %, overwatch danger and target value, with a small random factor.

## 8. Squad (level 1)

The game picks 4 of 6 classes at random, with no repeats. Random spread: HP ±2, Aim ±1, Will ±1.
Every soldier carries a dagger (1d4+2, silent) and 1 fire flask.

| Class | HP | Aim | Def | Armor | Mob | Will | Weapon | Abilities (cooldown) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vanguard | 13 | +4 | 11 | 1 | 6 | +1 | Warblade 2d6+2, melee. Throwing axes 1d6+1, range 1–4, 2 uses. | Charge (3): move + melee. Cleave (passive): a kill hits an adjacent enemy for 1d6. |
| Ranger | 9 | +6 | 11 | 0 | 5 | +1 | Longbow 1d10+3, range 6–20, 2 AP | Far Sight (passive): shoots targets that allies see. Steady Aim (passive): overwatch has no disadvantage. |
| Warden | 14 | +3 | 12 | 0 | 5 | +2 | Repeating crossbow 1d8+1, range 2–8, 5 bolts | Volley (2): target Suppressed, uses 2 bolts. Bulwark (4): 3 tiles of high cover for 2 rounds. |
| Cleric | 10 | +3 | 11 | 0 | 6 | +3 | Light crossbow 1d6+2, range 2–8, 4 bolts | Heal (2): 2d4+2, adjacent. Ward (3): +2 Defense that flanking does not remove, 2 rounds. |
| Rogue | 9 | +4 | 12 | 0 | 7 | +1 | Throwing knives 1d6+1, range 1–6, silent | Sneak Attack (passive): +1d6 against flanked or Unaware targets. Vanish (4): concealed again if unseen. 2 smoke bombs. |
| Mage | 8 | +4 | 10 | 0 | 5 | +4 | Arcane Bolt 1d8+2, range 2–10, ignores armor | Fear (3): Will save or Frightened for 2 rounds. Blink (4): teleport up to 5 tiles to a visible tile. |

## 9. Enemies (base tier)

| Enemy | HP | Aim | Def | Armor | Mob | Will | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orc Warrior | 9 | +3 | 11 | 0 | 5 | +0 | Heavy crossbow 1d8+2, range 2–10 | Line unit. Uses cover and flanks. |
| Goblin Bomber | 6 | +2 | 12 | 0 | 6 | +0 | Fire flask 3×3: 3 damage + Burning | Telegraphs its area 1 turn early. |
| Ogre | 16 | +4 | 9 | 1 | 7 | −1 | Club 2d6+2, melee | Telegraphs its charge. The hit pushes the target 1 tile. |
| Hobgoblin Shieldwall | 12 | +2 | 13 | 1 | 4 | +1 | Spear 1d8, melee | Adjacent allies get Armor +1. |
| Shadow Elf Assassin | 8 | +5 | 13 | 0 | 6 | +2 | Twin blades 1d8+3, melee | Concealed until within 3 tiles or until it attacks. Advantage against a soldier with no ally within 3 tiles. |
| Orc Warchief | 14 | +4 | 12 | 1 | 5 | +2 | Greataxe 1d12+2 / javelin 1d6+2, range 2–6 | Group gets Aim +1. Horn range 30. On its death, the group is Shaken. |
| Whisperer | 8 | +3 | 11 | 0 | 5 | +4 | Psychic Lash 1d6+2, range 8, Will save for half | Dread (3): Frightened. Dominate (4): the AI controls a soldier and can attack allies. It ends when the Whisperer dies, after 2 rounds, or when an adjacent ally uses Rouse. |

Groups: at most 1 Ogre, Assassin or Whisperer per group. Groups of 3 or more have a Warchief. Enemy vision: 8 tiles.

## 10. Statuses

| Status | Effect | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Suppressed | Disadvantage on attacks. Moving triggers a reaction shot from the suppressor. | Until the suppressor's next turn |
| Frightened | Disadvantage on attacks. Cannot move closer to the source. | 2 rounds |
| Shaken | Disadvantage on attacks | 1 round |
| Burning | 2 damage at round end | 2 rounds |
| Dominated | The enemy AI controls the unit | See Whisperer |

Smoke (area, 2 rounds): attacks against units inside it have disadvantage.

## 11. Why brute force fails

| Brute-force play | What stops it |
|---|---|
| Walk in and shoot the first enemy you see | Reveal reactions and horns pull 2–3 groups onto you |
| Turtle in cover | Mind magic ignores cover. The Bomber destroys cover. The AI flanks. |
| Focus fire on the nearest enemy | The Warchief's horn and the Whisperer's Dominate punish wrong target order |
| Creep slowly with overwatch | Patrols find you. Noise and horns bring enemies from several sides. |
| Split up to flank everywhere | The Assassin hunts lone soldiers |

## 12. Presentation and tech

- Look: square grid with flat colors. Units are team-colored disks with icons from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, credit needed), an HP bar and AP dots.
- Log: every roll is written out in full.
- Strings: English and Russian dictionaries. The language comes from the browser, with an EN/RU switch that is remembered.
- Save: localStorage keeps the seed and the action log. Reload replays the log through the rules.
- Action log: each player action is a signed Nostr NIP-01 event, with a local key in localStorage, so the log can go to relays later.
- Tech: TypeScript in the browser, in 3 modules: `rules` (pure, seeded, no rendering), `ai`, and `view` (Canvas). Tests use Vitest.
- Balance: headless AI-against-AI simulations over thousands of seeds.
